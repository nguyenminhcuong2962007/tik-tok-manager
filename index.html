TikTok Growth Manager

A personal follower analytics dashboard

TikTok Growth Manager is a personal analytics tool built by Nguyen Minh Cuong. It runs locally on the owner's own computer and helps the owner track follower counts and growth of TikTok accounts they own.

How it works

  • The account owner clicks "Connect TikTok" and signs in through TikTok's official Login Kit.
  • With the owner's permission, the tool reads the account's display name, avatar and public statistics (follower, following, like and video counts).
  • The tool shows total followers, 7-day growth charts and a list of connected accounts.

What it does not do

  • It does not post, delete or edit any content on TikTok.
  • It does not collect data about other TikTok users.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties.
