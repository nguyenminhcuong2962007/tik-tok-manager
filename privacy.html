Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Data we access

Only after you authorize the Tool through TikTok Login Kit, it accesses:

  • Your TikTok display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic).
  • Your account statistics: follower count, following count, likes count and video count (scope user.info.stats).

2. How we use the data

The data is used only to show your own follower totals, growth charts and account list inside the Tool. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Data and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The Tool does not upload them to any external server operated by us.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to third parties.

5. Your control

  • Disconnect an account in the Tool at any time.
  • Delete the local database to remove all stored data.
  • Revoke access from your TikTok account settings.

6. Children

The Tool is not intended for users under 13, or under the minimum age required by TikTok in your region.
